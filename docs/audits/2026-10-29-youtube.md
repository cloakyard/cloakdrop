# October 29, 2026 — YouTube AV1 timing fix and dependency refresh

## Reproduction and result

The reported URL was `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7zfir0Ide0A`
(“Steve Jobs on Touch Screen MacBooks in 2010”). Its selected 720p rendition is
AV1 in MP4 with separate AAC audio. The direct video contains 1,333 frames at
30 fps over 44.433333 seconds. The user's completed MP4 contained all 1,333
frames but played them at 15 fps over 88.866667 seconds; its audio ended around
44.44 seconds. This was a mux timing error, not missing video transfer bytes.

AVFoundation accepted the AV1 track and reported a successful passthrough export.
That prevented the existing FFmpeg fallback from running. The native backend now
checks actual video format descriptions before both mux and remux, accepting
H.264/HEVC and handing other codecs to FFmpeg. It does not infer support from the
MP4 extension or the ability to load a track.

FFmpeg no longer uses `-shortest`, which could discard the end of the longer
track. Paired downloads now fail and retain both staged streams when no muxer
can combine them, instead of publishing video-only success and deleting audio.
The existing H.264/HEVC AVFoundation assembly behavior is unchanged.

The rebuilt sandboxed Debug app resolved the pasted URL with bundled yt-dlp
2026.10.26, offered the quality picker, downloaded 720p, and completed a Matroska
file with AV1 and AAC. Its duration is **44.489 seconds**, with **1,333 video
packets at 30 fps** and 1,916 audio packets. Decoding every video frame and
comparing frame MD5s against the direct source produced **1,333 identical frames**.
Encoded packet hashes differ across MP4/Matroska repackaging; decoded-frame
comparison is the relevant pixel-preservation evidence.

The corrected `Steve Jobs on Touch Screen MacBooks in 2010 (2).mkv` remains in the
user's Downloads as a deliverable, beside the original. Its completed record and
approximately 2.5 MB of transfer statistics remain. Existing files and download
records were not removed. No signed media URLs or extractor JSON are committed.
